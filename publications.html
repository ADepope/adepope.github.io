  1. 2026

    Joint modeling of whole-genome sequencing data for human height via approximate message passing

    A. Depope, J. Bajzik, M. Mondelli, M. R. Robinson

    JournalCell Genomics 6(5), 101162

    We develop gVAMP, an approximate message passing paradigm that fine-maps whole-genome sequence variants and gene burden scores conditional on all other measured DNA variation genome-wide, and show that the genetic architecture of height inferred from WGS data differs systematically from the one inferred from imputed SNPs.

    Joint Variable Selection in Proteomics Survival Models

    J. Bajzik*, A. Depope*, Y. Zolfimoselo, A. Sharipov, A. Lesayova, H. Klein, A. Richmond, S. Vernardis, A. Grauslys, S. Andrejev, A. Zelezniak, M. Ralser, R. E. Marioni, M. Mondelli, M. R. Robinson

    WorkshopICLR Workshop on Machine Learning for Genomics Explorations

    A scalable Bayesian framework for joint variable selection in time-to-event models. Applied to the UK Biobank Pharma Proteomics Project, it improves onset prediction by 26–33% relative to penalised Cox and baseline deep-learning approaches.
